# 🌍 Welcome to OoitGedacht SMP!

First things first: **this is a chill server.** Building, playing together, having a good time. Nothing is mandatory. 😊

The season follows a timeline in real-life weeks:

📅 **Week 1** (from Sat Oct 11) – Normal survival. Died? Just respawn.
🔥 **Week 2** (from Sat Oct 18) – The **Nether** opens.
🌌 **Week 3** (from Sat Oct 25) – The **End** opens.
💀 **Week 4** (from Sat Nov 1) – **Hardcore + the Finale.** If you die, you're a spectator until the end of the season.

🧱 The world has a border of **6000 blocks** that grows a tiny bit every in-game day.

Type `/stimeline` in-game to see where we are.
---
# ⚔️ The Finale (week 4) – just a fun closer!

The battle royale is **not mandatory**: it's a fun way to close the season, nothing more. You can also just keep building or watch.

🔻 Every evening at **20:00** (Dutch time) the world border shrinks (warning 10 minutes before, the shrinking takes half an hour). After 7 days it's only **50 blocks** wide.
🚪 The Nether and the End are closed during the finale.
🗡️ Kill someone and you get **their head** as a trophy.
⏰ If you're **offline for 24 hours** during the finale, you're out.
🧭 Coming back and standing outside the border? You get **30 minutes** to walk to it (the way is shown on screen). Walking the wrong way = damage!
⚠️ Better not log out far outside the play area in the last week 😉
🏆 The **last survivor wins** OoitGedacht SMP!

**After that?** The world border disappears, hardcore turns off, everyone comes back to life and we just **keep playing survival, without a border.** 🎉

# 🛠️ Good to know

🏠 **Lobby** – `/lobby` and `/survival`, or click the NPCs
🧗 **Parkour** – step on the start! `/parkour top` for the fastest times
🥊 **Duels** – `/duel <name>` or click the duel NPC. Your own items are safe, you get a kit and you never really die.
🏡 **Homes** – `/sethome [name]`, `/home`, `/homes` (max. 2)
🤝 **Teleport** – `/tpa <name>` and `/tpahere <name>` (stand still for a moment while teleporting)
🛏️ **Sleeping** – when half of the players sleep, it becomes morning.
🪙 **Nuggets** – right-click with a gold, copper or iron nugget for a mini game
🚫 The **mace** and **spears** are disabled.
🚩 **Problems?** `/report <player> <reason>` goes straight to the staff.

Have fun and see you on the server! 💚
